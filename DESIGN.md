# LingoMage design

## Reference and visual language

Follow the App² starter template and host slate palette. The reference is App²'s `app/src/main/assets/starter_template/css/style.css` and native `res/values/colors.xml`. Keep LingoMage's blue reading accents within that shared visual language. This document describes the current design; it does not establish usability or learning effectiveness.

Use the shared CSS tokens: `--paper` (#090d16), slate reader surface (#0f172a), `--card`, `--line`, `--field`, `--ink`, `--muted` (#94a3b8), `--accent`, and `--accent-strong` (#3b82f6). Use 18px card corners, 12px field/button corners, subtle borders and restrained elevation. Avoid introducing a separate reader color theme.

## Layout and controls

Create leads with an optional multiline Story idea (premise, genre, tone or format), then optional Topics to explore (domains, communication situations, concepts or expressions), then Language and Reading difficulty. Keep the learning-preferences setup notice beside the language fields. These story-first choices are product assumptions.

Keep the mobile-first Create, My stories and Settings navigation with icons above labels, matching the App² starter. Reserve space for the bottom bar and combine host and browser safe-area insets. Controls have at least 44px touch targets, visible keyboard focus and English accessible labels. Respect reduced motion. Use shared button and field classes instead of feature-specific replacements.

The reader is full-screen, with a centered column no wider than 780px and no top bar. Use the story title, then separate chapter/path navigation from text-size and furigana controls, letting the groups wrap on narrow screens. Android Back returns to My stories while the reader is open. Give canonical story text comfortable line spacing and keep Japanese readings as separate annotations. Glossary forms have a subtle underline and open a small anchored note on tap or keyboard activation; note controls must keep story text selectable. The note is a labelled nonmodal dialog popover with a 44px Listen icon beside each headword. Opening focuses the popover; Escape returns focus to its trigger. Listen speaks the canonical displayed headword, excluding readings, through App² speech with the story locale. Browser previews disable Listen with an App² availability explanation. Beneath the story, place equal-width Continue story and Rewrite chapter buttons, then the prompt panel when opened, then one Explanations section with saved cards newest first. Story deletion is available from My stories, rather than the reader.

Selecting text offers Translate, Explain and a leading Speak speaker icon in the LingoMage toolbar. The Speak icon uses the shared icon-button styling and an accessible “Speak selected text” label. Speak uses canonical selected text and the story locale through the existing App² speech wrapper; browser previews disable it with an availability explanation. In App², Copy and Speak remain in Android’s native selection menu; Speak uses the selected text’s language and Android’s configured voice. Keep the Explain toolbar available in every environment. Stop native playback when the reader closes or its chapter/version changes.

Long-press the non-text surface of a saved explanation card to select it; text inside the card remains available for native selection and context menus. Padding and gaps are selection surfaces. Use compact checkbox marks in 44px touch targets, a floating bottom bar with selection count, trash control and a mixed-state checkbox. The checkbox shows a minus for partial selection; activating it selects all explanations in the displayed chapter version. When all are selected it shows a checkmark; activating it clears selection and exits. Keep the bar centered within the reader, 12px above the bottom safe area, and reserve bottom scrolling space for the final card. The library selection toolbar retains its existing behavior. Tapping a non-text card surface or checkbox toggles it; text remains selectable while selection mode is active. Clearing the last selection exits the mode. Hide follow-up actions while selecting. Provide a keyboard-accessible Select explanation control on focus and let Escape exit selection. Preserve scroll position and focus as selection changes. Deleting selected explanations also removes their attached follow-ups.

On My stories, open a story with a normal tap and enter bulk selection with a long press or the accessible per-story Select story control, shown on keyboard focus. Show compact checkbox marks in 44px touch targets and a sticky selection toolbar in that mode; remove the global Select stories button and any permanently visible per-card delete control. The trash button opens an app-styled confirmation that names the selected story count and warns that chapters, versions and explanations are removed. Cancel preserves selection. Tapping a story card toggles it while selection is active; clearing the last selection exits the mode. These interaction choices are product assumptions, not evidence of usability or learning effects.

Selection checkboxes have 44px touch targets. Cancel a pending long press when the pointer leaves the card, moves beyond the hold threshold, is released or cancelled, or the page scrolls.

## Selection help and follow-ups

Explain acts on the exact canonical selection. Group saved cards under one Explanations heading, with separate bordered cards and an accent rule marking the relevant source excerpt. The model chooses a contiguous source excerpt and the app highlights only the user’s exact selection. Context translations are optional and appear beneath the excerpt when useful. Initial help is one short paragraph. English help uses the interface font; story text keeps its reading typography. Wrap long content without horizontal scrolling. Keep saved explanations visible while a request runs. Show a compact, cancellable loading panel above the cards for initial help and inside the matching card for details and examples; retain the question after a failed detail request. Examples remain inside their explanation card, separated by a divider.

Keep clear vertical spacing between explanation actions and any loading, error, or recovery content inserted into the same card. Continuation and rewrite show the same compact status-card style beneath the edit panel, with an action-specific heading and Cancel request. The card appears from request preparation through completion and follows its initiating story when the reader changes. Keep the current chapter visible, disable editing during generation, retain failed prompts, and preserve explicit save/release recovery.

More detail reveals a labelled, one-line, optional question field with Cancel and More detail. Opening or cancelling the field must not request AI. Enter submits; an empty question requests general detail on the same selection. Display each submitted question with its saved follow-up response. Detail responses remain text in storage and appear as saved follow-ups with paragraphs, bold, italic and inline code rendered using DOM elements and text nodes. Unsupported formatting and HTML remain literal; Generate examples directly requests three short examples of the same contextual function. Each example shows a highlighted relevant form and an English translation under an Examples heading. Both actions remain available; additional follow-ups append in order. Use text nodes for all learner and model content. Keep request cancellation and explicit save/release retry controls available.

## Verification

Every interface or copy change requires a visual screenshot inspection at desktop and narrow mobile widths before completion. Verify spacing, wrapping, hierarchy, clipping and touch targets, including loading/error states when affected. A passing DOM or automated functional check does not verify layout quality. Record the screenshots/layouts actually inspected and any remaining device-only checks.

Keep helper copy concise and visually grouped with its field: a 6px field-to-helper gap and a 22px gap after each complete Create field group. Labels, controls and helpers share a group; generic field bottom margins must not push helpers toward the next label. Textareas need internal vertical padding.

Check desktop and narrow mobile layouts, visible furigana, large reading text, keyboard focus, disclosures and follow-up submission in the deterministic development server. Mock checks establish UI behavior only. Host insets, Android keyboard and selection behavior require App² device verification.

On My stories, swipe a card left at least 60px to reveal an 80px-wide, full-height red delete area with a white trash icon. Slide the entire padded slate reading surface over this area, keeping the card corners clipped; do not move text alone. Recognize horizontal intent only when horizontal movement exceeds vertical movement by 1.5×; preserve vertical scrolling and cancel long press during a swipe. Mouse dragging uses the same gesture. Only one card is revealed; a rightward swipe, outside tap, opening a story, filter change or bulk selection closes it. The trash control opens the app-styled confirmation naming the story and warning that all chapters, versions and explanations are permanently removed. Cancel receives initial focus. Hover and keyboard focus do not reveal the control: this gesture-only discoverability is a product assumption and limits keyboard access to single deletion; keyboard-accessible bulk selection remains available.

Continuation and rewrite prompt labels show optional. Blank continuation follows the existing narrative; blank rewrite creates a fresh telling of the same main events. Keep concise placeholders explaining that ideas may be multilingual or blank. Settings helpers and examples are brief, with 6px to their field and 22px before the next labelled field; reading-band summaries retain Japanese kana and Russian Cyrillic prerequisites.

Create shows one brief language-input note above the story fields: “Write in English, your learning language, or a mix.” Both fields reference it for accessibility. Story idea gently invites writing in the learning language. Chapter prompt placeholders also allow ideas in any language or a blank submission. Avoid repeating the note under each field or implying that these inputs provide writing corrections.


Story paths preserve alternate histories. Show a labelled Story path selector above chapter navigation only when more than one path exists; name paths Original and Alternate N. Chapter navigation contains only that path's pinned versions. Rewriting switches to a new path ending at the rewritten chapter and preserves the source path's later chapters. Show the fork chapter and source path beneath navigation. Continue appends after the selected path's ending even when an earlier chapter is displayed. Keep reading positions separately per path within the page session. Disable path switching during generation; navigation within the path may remain available. Preparing story context uses the existing cancellable status card before chapter generation. Retain the prompt and completed memory checkpoints after cancellation or failure. These interaction choices are product assumptions.


Story actions is a compact disclosure beside chapter navigation, available with one or multiple paths. It contains Remove Chapter N onward (or Remove Chapter N for the final chapter), plus Delete this path when alternatives exist or Delete story for the sole path. The disclosure is keyboard accessible; Escape closes it and restores focus. Scroll, resize, outside interaction and chapter changes dismiss it. Pending AI work or unsaved/unreleased results disable destructive actions with a short explanation.

Removal uses app-styled confirmation with Cancel initially focused. Name the selected path and exact chapter range, explain preservation on other paths, and warn that exclusively unused content is permanently deleted. Chapter 1 removal deletes that path; removing the only path uses whole-story confirmation. Successful truncation displays the preceding chapter; deleting a path opens the oldest remaining path at its beginning, preserving its name. Hide the path selector when only one path survives. Keep confirmations available on failed saves with an inline retry explanation. These choices are product assumptions; no trash or undo is offered.

Reader AI requests also show a floating bottom status bar for explanations, details, examples, continuation and rewrite. Keep it above safe-area insets, reserve scrolling space and lift selection toolbars above it. Show Cancel request during inference and a persistent Ready / View result after saving and releasing the request. View result opens the originating chapter/version and scrolls to its explanation or chapter heading. Do not scroll to loading content automatically. Errors remain dismissible; save and release failures retain explicit recovery actions. Status follows its initiating story. These interaction choices are product assumptions.

Translate opens the configured device translation handler after a deliberate tap, forwarding only canonical selected text. The external tool chooses source and target languages; English output is not guaranteed. Check translation availability when selection shows the toolbar or changes the selected passage, without startup or post-launch checks. Cache confirmed availability; temporary check failures preserve it and stale/background responses are ignored. Disable Translate before availability is confirmed, when no handler is confirmed, or while a launch is pending. Show launch errors without retrying automatically.

The reader title has the same 44px Listen speaker icon as glossary notes. It reads the complete displayed chapter's canonical story text in its story locale, excluding title, furigana, glossary and explanations. Long chapters play as sequential bounded utterances. Native Stop, replacement speech, reader closure and chapter/version changes interrupt playback; never resume subsequent chunks after cancellation. Browser previews disable Listen with the existing App² availability explanation.

Japanese ruby readings belong above contiguous kanji runs, with compounds kept together. Written kana, okurigana and punctuation remain plain canonical text. Normalize legacy mixed annotations for display only when their readings align uniquely; preserve original data and avoid guessing ambiguous pronunciations.

Story length uses a three-position Settings slider with visible Short/Medium/Long labels, an accessible selected-value announcement and concise range helper. Length changes future chapters independently of difficulty. Checkpoint recovery appears in Create, Settings and the originating reader; show stage-specific status, Cancel while inference is active, and Resume / Save / finish plus Discard when idle. Never expose partial prose as a completed story or move inline glossary notes into a separate section.

Reader text selection shows a compact floating popover (dark card, 14px radius, soft shadow, 44px targets) centered above the selected text and clamped to the viewport. When there is no room above it moves below the selection, with extra clearance on touch devices so it does not cover native selection handles. It repositions on scroll and resize. Actions are a Speak icon button first, then Translate and Explain as text labels, then a Copy icon button (accessible labels “Copy selected text” and “Speak selected text”). Select all is removed; the reader title row has a Copy chapter icon beside Listen that copies the displayed chapter's canonical text, without title or furigana. The app has a dark theme only, so the popover follows it. These interaction choices are product assumptions.
