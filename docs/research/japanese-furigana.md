# Japanese reading support: furigana

Review date: 2026-10-09. Status: preliminary discovery record; original source
verification remains incomplete.

Kirwan, L. (2003). *The role of furigana in Japanese script for second language
learners of Japanese*. University of Queensland thesis.
[DOI: 10.14264/106481](https://doi.org/10.14264/106481).

Access: Consensus abstract/record only. The DOI could not be retrieved in this
session; original full text, sample sizes, and degree metadata were not verified.
Do not present this record as a completed primary-source review.

The indexed abstract describes four studies of beginning and advanced Japanese
learners and a learner survey. It reports limited facilitation for some beginners
and different outcomes for more advanced learners, depending on prior knowledge
and writing-system background. These are provisional summaries pending original
verification, not settled conclusions about always-visible annotation.

Decision: furigana remains always visible in the initial beginner reader because
the user explicitly requires it. This evidence candidate does not establish its
optimal display policy, long-term effects, or transfer to English-speaking
learners reading AI-generated stories.

Next review: verify the thesis, seek more recent controlled studies, distinguish
immediate comprehension from kanji acquisition, and examine annotation accuracy
and readability. No app-specific learning effects have been measured.
