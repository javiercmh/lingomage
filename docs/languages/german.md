# German

Shared rules: [languages README](README.md).

## Current requirements

- Correct noun capitalization, umlauts and ß; correct articles, case, agreement, word order and separable verbs.
- Glossary notes link inflected story forms to their dictionary headword (the validator checks that declared forms occur in the text, not that they belong to the lemma).

## Earlier designs

- **Android prompt 2026-10-09.1:** 100–150 words, short main clauses, common present-tense patterns, explicit subjects, few subordinate clauses. These were product assumptions, not an evidence-based grammar progression; retired with numeric quotas under PED-004. Shared rationale: [reading and glossing review](../research/reading-and-glossing.md).
- **2026-10-10:** reading bands with free-text self-assessment (e.g. familiar patterns, word-order difficulties); replaced by CEFR targets (2026-10-13).

## Open questions

- Noun entries: article, gender, plural, capitalization in notes.
- Matching chosen dictionary forms to inflected story forms.
- Grammar progression: cases, verb position, separable verbs, tenses.
- Glossary treatment of compounds and context-specific meanings.

## Content review criteria

Agreement, articles, capitalization, word order, natural vocabulary, glossary accuracy and readability for the level; vocabulary coverage judged without relying only on exact dictionary-form matches.
